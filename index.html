About

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, in the Security Research Group, advised by Dr. Pubali Datta. My research addresses security and privacy challenges in software systems and online platforms, with a focus on cloud and network systems security, usable security and privacy, and agentic AI.

I pursue two complementary directions: (1) scalable and robust methods for securing large-scale systems, including AI-assisted approaches for analyzing, generating, and enforcing security policies; and (2) human-centered studies of security and privacy on online platforms, to support safer decision-making.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in 2024, and then worked as a lecturer before joining UMass Amherst in Fall 2025.

Research

Securing systems with AI

Using LLMs to generate, validate, and repair access control policies, and studying authorization for agentic AI systems.

Usable security and privacy

Large-scale and mixed-methods studies of scams, synthetic media, and app privacy on the platforms students use.

Reparo: Synthesizing and Validating Access Control Policies

First authorUnder review at a top-tier conference

A multi-LLM framework that generates, validates, and repairs access control policies in Cedar, OpenFGA, and Rego from repository artifacts. Evaluated on 228 real-world repositories.

Scams Among U.S. College Students

First authorUnder review at a top-tier conference

LLM-assisted analysis of 26K+ posts across 83 college subreddits to characterize student scam experiences, harms, and responses. A follow-up survey and interview study on scams targeting international workers in the U.S. is in design.

Understanding Synthetic Politics on Reddit

First authorUSENIX Security '26 poster

A mixed-methods study of how political deepfakes spread and how communities react to them in r/politics and r/futurology.

Privacy of Student-Used Apps and Authorization for Agentic AI

First authorOngoing

Measuring the privacy practices of 50+ apps used by students, and exploring Cedar-based authorization for agentic AI systems.

Undergraduate research (BUET)

LLM-based code review and refinement using call-graph metadata and QLoRA fine-tuning, advised by Dr. Anindya Iqbal and Dr. Toufique Ahmed. Detection of attack behavior in DARPA OPTC and TC E3 logs using provenance graphs, advised by Dr. Shohrab Hossain and Dr. Shahrear Iqbal.
