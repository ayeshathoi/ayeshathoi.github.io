About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, in the Security Research Group, advised by Dr. Pubali Datta. My research addresses security and privacy challenges in software systems and online platforms, spanning cloud and network systems security, usable security and privacy, and agentic AI.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in 2024, and worked as a university lecturer before starting my Ph.D. in Fall 2025.

Research

Securing systems with AI

  • Reparo: a multi-LLM framework that generates, validates, and repairs access control policies (Cedar, OpenFGA, Rego) from repository artifacts, evaluated on 228 real-world repositories.
  • Agentic AI authorization: exploring Cedar-based authorization for agentic AI systems.

Usable security and privacy

  • Student scams: LLM-assisted analysis of 26K+ posts across 83 college subreddits; a survey and interview study on scams targeting international workers in the U.S. is in design.
  • Synthetic media: how political deepfakes spread and are received on r/politics and r/futurology.
  • App privacy: measuring the privacy practices of 50+ student-used apps.

Undergraduate research, BUET

LLM-based code review and refinement (undergraduate thesis; advisors Dr. Anindya Iqbal and Dr. Toufique Ahmed). We combined static program metadata (function call graphs) and natural-language summaries (CodeT5-base) with BM25-retrieved few-shot examples and QLoRA fine-tuning, using GPT-4o, GPT-3.5-turbo, Gemini-1.0-pro, Llama-3, and Code-Llama on the CodeReviewer dataset. The approach surpassed the state-of-the-art CodeReviewer model, exceeding 90% BLEU. Supported by a RISE-BUET research grant.

Attack detection in system logs (advisors Dr. Shohrab Hossain and Dr. Shahrear Iqbal). We built provenance graphs and reverse trees from the DARPA OPTC and TC E3 logs (stored in PostgreSQL), generated event traces, and used LLMs to classify behavior as benign or malicious.
